Grip – Privacy Policy

Developer: Kealtie · Effective date: 14 October 2026

Grip (“the app”) shows large playback controls for your music apps on the Android lock screen. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it. In short: what you play and your listening history stay on your phone. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which receives device data to show and measure them; Grip Pro has no ads.

What the app accesses

With your permission (Notification access), the app reads the media sessions of apps that are playing audio, such as music and podcast apps. This includes the title, artist, album artwork, playback position and playback state of the current track, and the name of the app that is playing it. The app uses this to display the controls, to send commands you give (play, pause, skip, seek) back to that app, and to keep your listening history (see below).

The app does not read the content of your notifications, even though Android groups this permission under “Notification access”.

What the app stores

The app stores its settings on your device: whether the lock screen player is turned on, the Grip Pro appearance you chose (color, seek bar and clock style), the apps you switched off for the lock screen (their package names), and whether Grip Pro is unlocked.

Unless you turn it off, the app also keeps a listening history on your device: for each song you listened to, its title, artist, a small copy of the artwork, the app it played in, the date and time, and the name of the Bluetooth or USB audio device it played on (for example your car or headphones), if any. A song is only added after you have listened to a good part of it. The history is kept for the period you choose (one month, one year or until you delete it), can be cleared at any time in the app, and is excluded from Android backups and device transfers, so it never leaves your phone. To let you resume your music after the playing app was closed, the app also remembers the last track you played (its title, artist, artwork, position and the app it played in), likewise only on your device and outside backups; clearing your listening history removes it too. Other media information is kept in memory only while it is needed to show the player.

What the app collects or shares

The app itself never sends what you play, your listening history or your settings anywhere. Its only network access is the advertising described below. It contains no analytics or crash-reporting libraries of its own. The Google Play Billing library used for Grip Pro ships with a Google diagnostics component; the app removes its upload channel, so it sends nothing.

Advertising

The free version shows one ad in the listening history, provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google LLC elsewhere). Ads are never shown on the lock screen, and Grip Pro has no ads at all: for Grip Pro owners the advertising software never starts.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and sends to Google: your device’s IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), your device’s advertising ID and app set ID, how you interact with the ads (for example views and taps), and diagnostic information such as app launch time. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. The app does not give Google, or anyone else, what you play or your listening history.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time under “Ad privacy choices” in the app’s privacy section (on the Lock screen page). Without consent, Google shows non-personalized or limited ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads).

Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Purchases

Grip Pro is an optional one-time purchase sold through Google Play that, among other things, removes the ads. Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy. The app only receives confirmation from the Google Play Store app on your device that you own Grip Pro; it never sees your payment details or your Google account.

Other permissions

Display over other apps lets the player appear on the lock screen when you wake your phone. The app never unlocks your phone; unlocking always goes through your own PIN, pattern, fingerprint or face unlock.

On Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO phones the app may ask you to allow showing on the lock screen and opening windows from the background, for the same purpose.

Children

The app is not directed at children. Apart from the advertising data described above, it does not process personal data of anyone.
